Q1 REVENUE AND PERFORMANCE SUMMARY

Q1 revenue reached $5.2M, representing a 12% year‑over‑year increase and a 4% sequential growth from Q4. The top three products by revenue were Product A ($1.8M, 35% of total), Product B ($1.2M, 23%), and Product C ($0.9M, 17%). Product A continues to outperform, driven by strong enterprise adoption, while Product C saw a slight decline due to increased competition.

Regional performance varied significantly. North America contributed 48% of total revenue with a growth rate of 15%, propelled by successful upsell campaigns. Europe grew 8% to $1.4M, hindered by supply chain bottlenecks in the UK. APAC was the standout region, surging 20% to $1.1M, led by new partnerships in Japan and Australia. Latin America remains small but grew 22% from a low base.

Gross margin for Q1 was 58%, down 2 percentage points from Q4, mainly due to higher component costs. We expect margins to recover in Q2 as new supplier contracts take effect. Sales pipeline for Q2 is strong, with a forecast of $5.8M. Key initiatives include launching Product D in Europe and expanding the sales team in APAC. Overall, Q1 sets a solid foundation for the year, though we must monitor inventory levels for top‑selling SKUs to avoid stockouts.
