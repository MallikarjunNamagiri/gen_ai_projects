FINANCIAL KPIS – Q1

Gross margin for Q1 was 62%, a slight decline from 64% in Q4 due to increased raw material costs. Operating expenses (OPEX) totalled $1.2M, representing 23% of revenue, which is within budget. The largest OPEX categories are R&D (38%), Sales & Marketing (32%), and G&A (30%).

Net income was positive at $0.4M, yielding a net margin of 7.7%. Revenue growth of 12% YoY and 4% QoQ continues to trend above industry averages. Cash runway, based on current burn rate, stands at 18 months, providing a comfortable buffer for strategic investments.

Key financial health indicators: current ratio is 2.1, debt‑to‑equity is 0.3, and return on equity (ROE) is 18%. We are in a strong position to pursue growth initiatives. However, we must monitor gross margin erosion; a further 2% drop would significantly impact net income. To stabilise margins, we are exploring alternative suppliers and increasing prices for premium features by 5% starting Q2. Additionally, we plan to cap OPEX growth at 5% for the remainder of the year, ensuring that revenue growth outpaces expense growth. Overall, the financial outlook remains positive, with a projected Q2 revenue of $5.6M and net income of $0.5M.
